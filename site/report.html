Summary

The score is a weighted blend of three components, each a 0 to 100 favorability reading built from percentiles.

All readings

Today's value with its percentile, where it stood one month, three months and one year ago, and the spread of its own history. Twelve-month momentum is reported as a quintile because its history holds few independent observations.

History explorer

Any series over time. For the ten metrics you can switch between the raw value and its point-in-time percentile.

Breadth by component

The individual sector and country ETFs behind each breadth reading. Weights are approximate and fixed (see caveats).

Leverage simulation across lookbacks

Simulated daily-reset funds over several trailing periods, as cumulative returns. "Simple multiple" is the unlevered return times the leverage; the gap between it and the simulated figure is the compounding effect.
